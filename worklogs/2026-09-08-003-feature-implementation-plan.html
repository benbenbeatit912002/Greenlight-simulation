Decisions and assumptions

M1-M4 form the first demonstration release. Country is descriptive metadata, not an automatic model calibration. Weather alone cannot identify greenhouse parameters. Soil-temperature assumptions, outdoor CO2 and unknown timezone must be visible. Retain scientific-only results; older strategy references to approximation fallback are stale. M5 requires separately supplied and authorized observations.
